# Sturdy Statistics Deep Dive Extension

**Transform your Google searches and academic papers into organized, visual knowledge maps.**

The [Sturdy Statistics](https://sturdystatistics.com) Deep Dive extension integrates directly with your browser workflow to optimize your research process. When you search Google or view academic papers, kick off a Deep Dive that downloads massive amounts of content and organizes it. Go to our [Deep Dive Search page](http://sturdystatistics.com/deepdive-search) explore our Deep Dive Gallery.

## What It Does

**Google Deep Dive**: When launching from a Google search, the extension will pull the full content from the top 300 Google search results, trains a model and presents it in an interactive sunburst visualization. See time trends for each topic and instantly access the top excerpts for any area of interest.

**Citation Network Deep Dive**: When viewing a research paper, the extension will download metadata from every paper that cites or is cited by your current paper. Organizes the entire citation network by content themes, letting you explore the full academic conversation around any topic.

## Key Features

- **Visual Topic Maps**: Interactive sunburst charts show content organized by themes and subtopics
- **Time Trend Analysis**: Track how topics evolve over time across your data
- **Instant Excerpts**: Pull up the most relevant quotes and passages for any topic with one click
- **Reliable Results**: Uses Bayesian modeling instead of neural networks—no hallucinations, just consistent analysis
- **Fast Mode**: Hold "f" while clicking to run 2-5x faster with streamlined data collection

## Supported Sites

**Google**: Full search result analysis

**Academic Papers**: arXiv, PubMed, DOI links, Semantic Scholar

## Privacy

The extension stores zero data and accesses no identifying information. We only read your current URL to construct relevant Deep Dive links. Complete privacy details in our [Privacy Policy](https://github.com/Sturdy-Statistics/DeepDiveExtension/blob/main/privacy_policy.md).
